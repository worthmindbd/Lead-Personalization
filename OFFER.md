## Company Summary

Aevora Media is a **video post-production and VFX studio**. The practice centers on turning client-supplied footage into finished assets: brand films, long-form creator content, paid social ads, corporate pieces, and platform-native short-form.

**Positioning (from site metadata):** Global post-production and VFX; cinematic brand films, motion graphics, and performance-oriented visual content.  
**Base:** Dhaka, Bangladesh (listed in structured site data). Work is delivered for international clients.

**Primary tools cited on site:** Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve.

---

## What We Do (Scope)

### Post-production core

- Editorial and storytelling (pacing, structure, retention-focused cuts)
- Motion design (2D/3D graphics, kinetic type, data visualization, intros)
- VFX and CGI (compositing, green screen, environment work, photorealistic renders)
- Color grading (DaVinci Resolve) and sound (mix, master, sound design)
- Short-form and vertical content (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)
- Authority / talking-head and long-form YouTube-style content
- Ad and conversion creative (hooks, variants, platform-native cuts)
- AI-assisted production where needed (e.g. generated B-roll via Veo 3 / Runway, AI voiceover) — called out on project tiers

### Deliverable types (service marquee on site)

Brand films · Motion graphics · VFX and compositing · 3D animation · Sound design · Color grading · Aerial cinematography (in finishing context) · Social reels

### Formats and use cases implied across tiers

- Long-form documentary / corporate edits (roughly 3–5 minutes and longer “pillar” work)
- YouTube deep-dives, VSLs, promos
- Meta / social ad suites with multiple hook variants for testing
- Trailers, teasers, thumbnails, distribution kits
- Enterprise and technical 3D (medical, architectural, tech categories mentioned on top tier)

## What We Do Not Do (Not stated on marketing pages)

The public website does **not** spell out exclusions. Operationally, the studio is framed as **post-production**: clients are expected to **provide raw media and briefs**. Filming, scripting, and VO are not offered as standalone services on the site; AI voice and stock/generative visuals are used on some packages when footage is missing.
